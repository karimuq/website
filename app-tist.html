About the App

TIST (There Is Still Time) is a minimalist Android application designed for anyone committed to maintaining sobriety and building lifelong self-discipline. Built with a strict offline-first philosophy, all data remains strictly on your device — no account required, no remote servers, and zero internet connection needed.

The app tracks your sober streak from your chosen start date, displaying a live timer down to the second. As you reach key recovery milestones (24 hours, 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year), milestone badges unlock with encouraging messages to celebrate your resilience. If a setback or relapse occurs, you can log it candidly and restart your counter while preserving a complete history of past streaks and personal progress.

How to Use

Building your personal recovery streak with TIST takes just a few simple steps:

1

Set Clean Date

Pick the exact date and time your sobriety or self-improvement journey began to initialize the live clock.

2

Track Real-Time Milestones

Watch your streak count live in days, hours, and seconds. Celebrate unlockable milestone badges at each stage.

3

Log Reflections & Resets

If a slip or relapse happens, log candid reflection notes and restart your counter without losing past streak data.

4

100% Offline Resilience

All timestamps, milestone progress, and notes remain encrypted and stored locally on your device.

Key Capabilities

Live Streak Timer

Real-time countdown showing days, hours, minutes, and seconds of continuous sobriety.

Milestone Progression

Unlockable milestone badges at critical checkpoints to celebrate progress and maintain momentum.

Relapse & Reset Logging

Log relapses with optional reflection notes and restart your counter without losing history.

100% Offline & Private

No accounts, no cloud sync, and zero tracking. Your personal data stays entirely on your device.
