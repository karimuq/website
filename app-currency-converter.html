About the App

Currency Converter is a reliable financial utility engineered for international travelers, digital freelancers, and everyday users who need instant currency conversions without friction. The app provides a clean, tactile keypad with real-time rate computations as you type.

Designed with a privacy-first, offline-ready architecture, all favorite pairs, base preferences, and history logs remain exclusively stored on your device. The app caches the latest exchange rates locally, guaranteeing full conversion capability whether you're mid-flight or roaming in areas without data connectivity.

How to Use

Converting global currencies takes only a few quick gestures:

1

Select Base Currency

Choose your primary source currency from the curated worldwide list with swift instant search.

2

Enter Conversion Value

Type values using the built-in mathematical calculation keypad for instantaneous addition and splits.

3

Multi-Currency Output

View live converted valuations across all your selected watch-list currencies simultaneously in real time.

4

Convert Offline

Rates are cached locally so conversions remain fully operational even without internet connectivity.

Key Capabilities

Multi-Currency Conversion

Convert your entered amount into multiple target currencies simultaneously with instant live calculation.

Offline Rate Cache

Stores recent exchange rates locally so you can calculate conversions without an active internet connection.

Favorite Watchlists

Pin and reorder your preferred currency pairs to the top for swift one-tap calculations.

Integrated Math Keypad

Perform addition, subtraction, percentages, and multipliers directly inside the converter input field.
