2. Local User Data (Health, Utility & Finance Data)

We operate under a strict local-first privacy architecture. All sensitive personal logs, numeric inputs, and records entered by you remain 100% on your device:

  • Sobriety & Addiction Counters: Clean start dates, relapse history, checkpoint milestones, and reflection notes.
  • Health & Fitness Data: Body Mass Index (BMI) measurements, height, weight metrics, workout tallies, and daily 75 Hard checklists.
  • Financial & Utility Inputs: Stock watchlists, investment positions, custom counters, unit conversion histories, and local app preferences.
Zero Remote Developer Databases

The developer does not operate, host, or maintain any external databases or cloud servers to collect or review this personal input. No registration, user accounts, or passwords are ever required.

In addition, sensitive health applications explicitly disable automated Android cloud backups (android:allowBackup="false") so your habit logs and body measurements are not automatically mirrored to Google Drive without your active consent.

3. Location Permissions (GPS Speedometer)

Certain utility applications published under this account — specifically our GPS Speedometer & Odometer utility — request Android location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to deliver their primary functionality.

100% On-Device GPS Processing

Real-time GPS satellite coordinates are processed strictly locally on your physical device to compute instantaneous speed, heading, altitude, and distance traveled.

  • No Server Transmission: Your live coordinates and trip history are never transmitted to, recorded on, or shared with external developer servers.
  • No Background Surveillance: Location access runs strictly while you actively use the speedometer and halts the moment the app is closed.
  • Zero Location Monetization: We do not broker, sell, or license user GPS coordinates to location aggregators, data brokers, or advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To maintain free access to our applications and support continuous development, our mobile apps and Unity games may integrate the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, provided by Google LLC.

The Google AdMob SDK automatically collects and shares certain technical device identifiers and interaction data to serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements, manage frequency capping, and prevent invalid ad traffic and fraud:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and vendor device hardware identifiers.
  • Network & Coarse Location: IP address, device carrier, general country/city region, and connection speeds.
  • Diagnostic & Usage Telemetry: Ad impressions, user clicks, session timestamps, and crash diagnostic logs.
Sensitive Data Isolation Guarantee

Your sobriety start dates, BMI calculations, stock watchlists, and GPS route data are strictly walled off from the advertising SDK. No personal logs are ever shared with or accessible to Google AdMob.

Google Partner Policy Link

To understand how Google collects and uses information when you interact with apps using Google services, please consult Google's official privacy terms:

How to Opt Out or Reset Your Android Advertising ID

You can disable ad personalization or reset your advertising ID at any time directly through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device Settings app.
  2. Select Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID to remove personalized tracking identifiers entirely, or tap Reset advertising ID to generate a clean, unlinked ID.

6. User Data Retention & Deletion Rights

In full accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, users must have complete autonomy to permanently delete their data. Because all application data is stored locally on your device with no remote accounts, you have direct, instantaneous control:

1
In-App Deletion

You can reset or delete any individual tracker, sobriety counter, BMI entry, or watchlist item directly within the app settings.

2
Clear Storage in Android Settings

Open Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This immediately and permanently deletes all local databases, preferences, and cached records.

3
Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling the application from your device permanently purges all local storage and sandboxed directories immediately. No orphan records remain on any external server.

8. Data Security & Storage Isolation

We employ standard industry security protocols to protect your device data:

  • Android Application Sandboxing: Android isolates app storage so that other installed applications cannot read your private local databases without root permissions.
  • HTTPS / TLS Encryption: All third-party advertising SDK and web asset requests are encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS transport security.
  • Zero Remote Server Risk: Because your personal logs and metrics are not held in a centralized web server database, your data is not susceptible to remote cloud breaches.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Under major data protection frameworks (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area / UK and CCPA/CPRA in California), users maintain fundamental rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view all your stored metrics and tracking logs inside the apps at all times.
  • Right to Portability: Where supported (e.g. TIST), you can export a full JSON copy of your data directly to your device.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can delete all your records instantly by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can opt out of personalized ads via Android OS settings and revoke location/notification permissions anytime.
