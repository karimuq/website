1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Your privacy is an absolute priority. TIST — Sobriety & 75 Hard Tracker (referred to as "TIST", "the App", or "we", Application ID: com.tist.sobriety) was designed from the ground up as a private, local-first utility.

We firmly believe that sensitive personal journeys — such as sobriety streaks, relapse records, mental discipline routines, and daily 75 Hard fitness checklists — belong exclusively to you. The app functions without mandatory accounts, without cloud databases, and without remote servers capturing your habits.

Zero Backend Architecture

The developer does not operate or maintain any remote server or user database for TIST. All sobriety counters, journal entries, and challenge check-ins are saved solely on your local device.

2. Information We Collect & Handle

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts list, or payment details.

A. Information You Provide Inside the App

When you use TIST, you may create, record, and save content within the app, including:

  • Sobriety & Habit Trackers: Habit/addiction titles, custom clean start dates and times, unlocked milestones, and target goals.
  • Relapse & Restart Logs: Timestamps of resets, optional reflection notes, and relapse triggers you choose to document.
  • 75 Hard Challenge Data: Daily completed check-offs (two 45-minute workouts, water intake, reading progress, clean diet adherence, and optional local progress photo records).
  • App Preferences: Notification reminder times, visual preferences, and sound effect toggles.

All of the information above is stored exclusively on your device's local storage and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any developer server.

B. Information Automatically Collected

TIST does not run background analytics SDKs or proprietary user tracking tools. However, depending on device configuration and the integration of Google Play Services or Google AdMob, standard technical device metrics may be processed by Google LLC as detailed in Section 5.

3. On-Device Storage Guarantee

TIST utilizes your device's secure local sandbox (specifically WebView localStorage / internal app sandbox files) to keep all your streaks and records intact across sessions.

  • No Cloud Sync: No automatic synchronization to third-party clouds or remote web services.
  • Android Cloud Backup Disabled: The application explicitly configures android:allowBackup="false" to ensure that your sensitive habit records are not automatically transmitted to Google Drive cloud backups without your knowledge.
  • Total User Control: You own your data. You decide when to export it, when to clear it, and when to delete it.

4. Device Permissions Requested

TIST requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions required to provide its core features. We never request permissions for your location, microphone, contacts, or phone state.

Permission Type Purpose & Usage
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional (Android 13+) Used strictly to trigger local milestone notifications and daily 75 Hard reminder alerts configured by you. Reminders run locally on device.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Normal Allows your device's AlarmManager to fire local notifications at the exact daily reminder time you schedule.
INTERNET Normal Required by the Android WebView bridge to render bundled application assets locally and to load authorized ad services from Google AdMob if active.
WRITE / READ EXTERNAL STORAGE Optional / Scoped Used solely when you explicitly initiate the Export Backup or Share Milestone Card action to save your JSON backup or image to your Downloads folder.

You may grant or revoke notification permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > TIST > Notifications.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To help support ongoing maintenance and provide the app free of charge to users, TIST may utilize third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob / Google LLC.

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect and receive certain technical information from your device in accordance with Google's policies, such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • IP address and coarse country/region location.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and frequency capping.
  • Diagnostic information and ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, error logs).
No Sensitive Health Data Shared With Ad Networks

Your sobriety start dates, relapse notes, habit labels, and 75 Hard checklist tasks are never shared with or made accessible to Google AdMob or any third-party ad network.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, monetize, or transfer your personal data, habit logs, or health check-ins to any outside entity.

Data is only shared in the following narrow circumstances:

  • Third-Party SDK Providers: As disclosed in Section 5, Google LLC processes standard technical and advertising metrics through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Legal Compliance: We will only disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation. Because the developer stores no user records on remote servers, no user tracker data exists on our end to provide.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Data Deletion Policy, users must have complete autonomy to delete their stored data. Because TIST does not require an online account or maintain user databases, you have full, instant, and permanent control over deleting all app data directly on your device.

You can delete your data through any of the following simple methods:

1
Delete Within the App

You can delete or reset any individual habit tracker or 75 Hard challenge at any time via the tracker options menu or reset button in the app interface.

2
Clear App Data in Android Settings

Navigate to Settings > Apps > TIST > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This immediately erases all locally saved records, habits, and preferences.

3
Uninstall the Application

Uninstalling TIST permanently purges all local databases, application files, and caches from your device. No residual copies remain on any remote server.

8. Manual Backup & Data Portability

TIST features a built-in, user-controlled Export & Import utility (located under Settings > Data in the app):

  • Export: Generates a standard plaintext .json file (e.g., tist-backup.json) containing your habit names, milestones, and reset logs, saved directly to your local device or shared via the standard Android share sheet.
  • Import: Allows you to restore or merge your own previously exported JSON file on a new device.
  • No Intermediary: This backup file travels exclusively through channels you choose (e.g., your own cable, email, or private drive). It is never sent to our servers.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TIST is designed as a personal habit and sobriety management tool intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in jurisdictions governed by the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or target personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

10. Data Security Measures

We employ robust technical safeguards designed to protect your privacy:

  • Android Application Sandboxing: Android isolates app storage so that other applications cannot access TIST's private database without root permissions.
  • Encrypted Communications: Any communication between the application runtime and third-party advertising endpoints utilizes industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • No Server Risk: Because your habit data is not stored in a centralized cloud database, it is not vulnerable to large-scale remote server data breaches.

11. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you hold specific privacy rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can access all your logged data at any moment inside the app.
  • Right to Portability: You can export a full copy of your data at any time via the built-in JSON export feature.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can instantly and permanently erase all data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You can disable advertising personalization through Android OS settings or revoke notification permissions.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
