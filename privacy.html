2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free distribution and ongoing maintenance across our mobile applications, our apps may integrate the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, provided by Google LLC.

The Google AdMob SDK automatically collects and processes certain device information and telemetry to serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements, manage ad frequency, and prevent invalid traffic and ad fraud. This data may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and vendor-specific hardware identifiers.
  • Coarse Location & Network Information: IP address, general country/region location, and connection type.
  • App Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad views, click interactions, session durations, and crash/diagnostic logs.

For detailed information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services, please review Google's official documentation:

→ Google's Privacy & Terms: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps

How to Opt Out or Reset Your Android Advertising ID

You have full control over personalized advertising directly through your Android device operating system:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Tap Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Select Delete advertising ID to remove personalized ad tracking entirely, or tap Reset advertising ID to generate a fresh, unlinked identifier.

3. Location Permissions (GPS Speedometer)

Certain utility applications published under this account — notably our GPS Speedometer & Odometer utility — require device location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to perform their advertised functions.

100% Local GPS Processing Guarantee

Real-time GPS satellite coordinates are used strictly locally on your device to calculate velocity, instantaneous speed, heading, and trip distance.

  • Zero Developer Transmission: Your location coordinates and location history are never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on external developer servers.
  • No Continuous Background Tracking: Location access operates strictly while the speedometer active session is running and stops immediately when you close the utility.
  • No Third-Party Location Selling: We never share, broker, or sell user location data to data brokers, advertising aggregators, or analytics providers.

4. Local User Data (Health, Utility & Finance Data)

Applications published by Karim Abdelnour prioritize offline utility and on-device data processing — including:

  • Currency Converter (com.currencyconverter.karim): Preferred base currencies, saved conversion pairs, offline calculator history, and cached exchange rate data.
  • BMI calculator (com.bmi.karim): User height, body weight, calculated Body Mass Index (BMI) records, history tracking, and weight goal metrics.
  • Speedometer (com.speedometer.karim): Real-time speed metrics, trip distance counters, and session top-speed records calculated on-device via GPS.
  • TIST: Sobriety Tracker (com.tist.sobriety): Sobriety start dates, live streak timers, milestone badge achievements, and personal relapse reflection entries.
  • Stock Tracker: Personal financial watchlists and local trade notes.

Zero-Server / Local-First Architecture

All personal records, numerical metrics, habits, and notes entered by the user are saved 100% locally on the user's device (utilizing private Android internal storage, SQLite databases, or sandboxed WebView storage).

  • The developer does not operate, host, or maintain any external databases or cloud servers to collect or review this personal input.
  • No user registration, account sign-up, or cloud login is required to access full functionality.
  • Android cloud backup is explicitly disabled (android:allowBackup="false") in sensitive health tools to prevent unsolicited cloud mirroring.

Complete User Data Deletion Rights

Because your data is housed solely on your physical device, you maintain unrestricted, unilateral control over deleting your information at any moment:

1
In-App Deletion

Delete, edit, or reset individual trackers, habit streaks, or stock watchlists directly inside the respective application interface.

2
Clear Storage via Android Settings

Open Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This permanently wipes all saved data, database files, and local preferences.

3
Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling the application from your Android device instantly and permanently destroys the app's sandboxed storage directory. No orphaned copies remain on any remote server.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement rigorous safeguards to safeguard user data integrity:

  • Operating System Sandboxing: All local files, preferences, and database records reside within the secure Android application sandbox, preventing unauthorized access by other installed applications.
  • Encrypted Network Transmission: Any network communication with authorized third-party endpoints (such as Google AdMob or Google Play Core libraries) is strictly enforced via industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Zero Retention on Remote Servers: Because we do not transmit or store personal health, financial, or location records on developer servers, there is no remote database susceptible to external breaches or unauthorized retention.
