1. App Identity & Overview

This document represents the official Privacy Policy for the Android application TIST — Sobriety & 75 Hard Tracker (Application ID: com.tist.sobriety), created and maintained by developer Karim Abdelnour.

TIST is built as a self-contained, offline-first personal tracker for sobriety streaks, milestones, relapse tracking, and 75 Hard daily fitness challenges. We hold a strict belief that personal recovery, habit logs, and self-improvement journeys must remain private to the device owner.

Zero Developer Servers

The developer operates no user accounts, no login portals, no analytics databases, and no cloud backends for TIST. All your data stays locally within your device.

2. User Data Handling

The developer does not collect, harvest, or transmit your Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

Data You Store Within TIST:

  • Sobriety Timers: Habit titles (e.g. alcohol, smoking, custom habit), clean start dates, and time milestones.
  • Relapse Records: Timestamps of resets, streaks achieved, and optional reflection notes.
  • 75 Hard Challenge Items: Daily completion checklists (two 45-minute workouts, hydration tracking, diet rules, reading goals, and optional local progress photo markers).
  • Custom App Preferences: Daily check-in reminder times, notification sound toggles, and display preferences.

All such information is stored in local sandboxed storage on your device and is never transmitted off your device to the developer.

3. 100% On-Device Storage

All information is held in the application's local WebView storage and Android sandboxed files.

  • No Cloud Sync: No automatic synchronization to any remote server or cloud database.
  • Explicitly Disabled Cloud Backup: TIST sets android:allowBackup="false" to ensure habit and relapse data is not copied silently to Google Drive cloud backups.
  • Data Ownership: You have complete, unrestricted ownership and custody of your records.

4. Device Permissions Requested

TIST only requests Android permissions strictly necessary for its advertised features:

Permission Type Purpose & Usage
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional (Android 13+) Allows TIST to send you scheduled local notifications for daily 75 Hard check-ins and sobriety milestones. All notifications run locally on the device without external push servers.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Normal Enables the Android system alarm manager to trigger your configured reminder notifications at your chosen exact time.
INTERNET Normal Required by the Android WebView runtime to load local app assets and connect to authorized Google AdMob ad networks.
STORAGE / DOWNLOADS Optional / Scoped Required only when you choose to export your data backup JSON file to your device's Downloads directory or share a progress card.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Advertising

To support free distribution and ongoing maintenance, TIST integrates advertising SDKs provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device data to deliver relevant ads and maintain service reliability:

  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version).
  • Approximate geographical location based on IP address.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for personalized or non-personalized advertising.
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, and crash diagnostics).
Privacy Safeguard

Your personal habit trackers, sobriety dates, and 75 Hard checklists are strictly isolated from advertising SDKs. No habit, relapse, or health data is ever accessible to Google AdMob.

Google Privacy Links:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Ad Personalization Controls: https://adssettings.google.com/

Opting Out / Resetting Advertising ID: You can manage or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.

  • Data processing by third parties is limited strictly to Google LLC for serving ads and Google Play platform services.
  • We do not share any data with data brokers, marketing agencies, or analytics firms.
  • Because no user records are stored on our servers, we have no user data to produce for third-party requests.

7. Data Retention & Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Google Play Developer Policy requires developers to state retention periods and provide clear data deletion mechanisms. Because all data in TIST is stored locally on your device with no remote account, you have immediate, direct control over deleting your data:

1
Delete Within the App

You can delete any tracker or reset your 75 Hard challenge directly inside the app using the tracker settings menu.

2
Clear App Data in Android Settings

Navigate to Settings > Apps > TIST > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This instantly erases all local database records and settings.

3
Uninstalling TIST

Uninstalling the application from your device permanently purges all application storage, local files, and cache immediately.

8. Manual Backup & Portability

TIST provides a built-in JSON export and import feature in Settings > Data:

  • You can export a complete snapshot of your trackers as a plain .json file (e.g. tist-backup.json).
  • You can restore or merge your data on another device without any external server or account requirement.
  • The export file is controlled solely by you and is never uploaded automatically to any server.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TIST is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can address the situation.

10. Data Security Measures

Your data security is guaranteed by design:

  • Local Device Sandboxing: Android's application sandbox prevents other apps from accessing TIST's local data.
  • Zero Remote Attack Surface: Because there is no centralized database holding user recovery logs, your data cannot be exposed through server database leaks.
  • Encrypted Connections: Any outbound ad requests use encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.

11. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

In accordance with global privacy frameworks (including the European GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: All your data is visible to you inside the app at all times.
  • Right to Portability: You can export all your data in standard JSON format anytime.
  • Right to Deletion: You can permanently wipe all records anytime directly from your device.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can disable advertising personalization via Android OS system settings.

12. Updates to This Policy
