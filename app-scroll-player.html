About the App

Scroll Player is a desktop media player focused on a scroll-driven interaction model. Instead of traditional scrubbing, you can scroll through video content naturally — making it easier to skim, review, or navigate long recordings.

It is built with Python and targets users who work with long-form video content such as lectures, interviews, or recordings — where quick navigation is more important than playback controls.

Features

Scroll Navigation

Scroll through video content with the mouse wheel for natural, fast navigation.

Playback Controls

Standard play/pause, volume, and seek controls alongside the scroll interface.

Format Support

Supports major video formats including MP4, MKV, AVI, and more.

Lightweight

Minimal resource footprint — no browser, no Electron, just a Python app.
