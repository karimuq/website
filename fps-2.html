  • Actions: Space Jump • R Reload • Shift Sprint

About the Game & Key Features

A 3D first-person shooter built in Unity with C#, featuring responsive gunplay, fluid movement, and intelligent enemy AI.

Weapon Gunplay

Custom recoil patterns, muzzle flashes, and fast reloading.

Fluid Movement

Responsive first-person movement with jump and sprint controls.

Enemy AI

Combat bots patrol arenas, track sightlines, and return fire.

Battle Arenas

Multi-level combat layouts designed with cover spots and clear sightlines.
