Speedometer

Closed Testing

A GPS-based mobile speedometer application for real-time speed tracking and trip velocity metrics. Designed for drivers, cyclists, and anyone who wants accurate on-device speed data.

Android Java GPS Utility Proprietary

About the App

Speedometer uses device hardware GPS to calculate and render your true velocity in real time. Built with a high-contrast digital gauge interface, it provides instantaneous visibility whether mounted on a car dashboard, bicycle handlebar, or handheld during outdoor fitness.

In addition to real-time velocity, the app aggregates trip statistics including maximum speed achieved, average trip velocity, and total distance traveled. All calculations are executed directly on your hardware without internet requirements or telemetry tracking.

How to Use

Getting accurate speed metrics takes only a moment:

1

Launch & Mount

Open Speedometer on your Android device and secure it in landscape or portrait orientation.

2

Enable GPS

Grant location permission to let hardware GPS connect directly to positioning satellites.

3

Select Unit

Switch between metric (km/h) and imperial (mph) speed measurements with a single tap.

4

View Trip Telemetry

Monitor your live speed, average pace, top speed reached, and total accumulated trip distance.

Key Capabilities

Real-Time Satellite Speed

Live GPS-derived speed display refreshed continuously with low latency.

Instant Unit Toggle

Seamlessly switch between km/h and mph at any point during active tracking.

Trip Session Statistics

Tracks peak maximum velocity, average journey speed, and total mileage.

100% Offline GPS Engine

Operates entirely via on-device GPS hardware without requiring cellular data.
