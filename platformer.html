About the Game & Key Features

A precision 2D platformer built in Unity with custom pixel art, responsive physics, and gamepad support.

Custom Pixel Art

Hand-drawn sprites, animations, and level tiles.

Precision Jumping

Coyote time and jump buffering make every hop consistent.

Level Hazards

Navigate moving platforms, spikes, and tight gaps.

Game Juice

Particle dust trails, landing impacts, and screen shake.
