ScreenSync

Concept

A screen-time accountability app concept that lets you share your daily phone usage with trusted friends and family to build healthier digital habits together.

Web App JavaScript Concept Productivity

About the Concept

ScreenSync is a design and UX concept exploring social accountability as a tool for reducing phone addiction. The core idea: sharing your screen time data with people you trust creates gentle social pressure to use your phone less.

This is an interactive web concept demonstrating the UI and core flows — a dashboard showing your daily usage, a friend feed showing their usage, and streak tracking for staying under your daily limit.

Concept Features

Usage Dashboard

Visual breakdown of daily screen time by app category and total hours.

Friend Feed

See your friends' daily screen time summaries to stay accountable together.

Goal Streaks

Set a daily screen time goal and track how many days in a row you hit it.

Privacy Controls

Choose exactly what you share and with whom — nothing is public by default.
