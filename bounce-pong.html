Controls

  • Player 1 (Left Paddle): W Up / S Down
  • Player 2 (Right Paddle): ↑ Up Arrow / ↓ Down Arrow
  • Restart Match: Press R
  • Pause Game: Press Tab, Esc, or P

About Bounce Pong & Key Features

A browser paddle game featuring angle-based ball physics and local two-player matches.

Angle-Based Bounces

Ball trajectory changes based on where it strikes your paddle.

Play in Browser

Runs directly in your browser with zero downloads.

Arcade Rounds

Quick rounds built for chasing high scores.

Local Versus

Play head-to-head matches against a friend on the same screen.
