About the App

Sober Tracker is a minimalist Android application designed for people working to maintain sobriety. Built with complete offline-first privacy in mind — all data stays on-device and no internet connection is ever required.

The app tracks your sober streak from a start date, displaying a live timer down to the second. As you reach key milestones (24 hours, 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year), milestone badges unlock with encouraging messages. If a relapse occurs, you can log it and restart your journey while keeping a history of past streaks.

Features

Live Streak Timer

Real-time countdown showing days, hours, minutes, and seconds of continuous sobriety.

Milestone Progression

Unlockable milestone badges at key checkpoints to celebrate your progress.

Relapse Logging

Log relapses with optional notes and restart your counter without losing history.

100% Offline

No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your data stays entirely on your device.
