About the Game & Key Features

A 3D vehicle physics prototype built in Unity with C#, focusing on realistic car handling and responsive racing controls.

Suspension Physics

Real-time spring and damper forces for lifelike weight transfer.

Tire Grip & Drifting

Custom tire friction curves for realistic cornering and slides.

Dynamic Camera

Chase cam adjusts distance and field of view as speed increases.

Lap Timers

Built-in checkpoint tracking and lap time counter.
