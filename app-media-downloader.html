About the App

Media Downloader provides a simple GUI on top of yt-dlp — one of the most capable open-source media download libraries. Instead of wrestling with command-line flags, you get a clean desktop interface where you paste a URL and hit download.

The app supports video and audio download in multiple formats and quality levels. It handles playlists, merges streams, and saves progress — all without a browser extension or account requirement.

Features

URL Paste & Download

Paste any supported URL and download video or audio in your chosen quality.

Format Selection

Choose between MP4, MP3, WebM, and other formats before downloading.

Playlist Support

Download entire playlists or channels with a single URL.

Progress Tracking

Live progress bar and speed indicator so you know exactly what is happening.
