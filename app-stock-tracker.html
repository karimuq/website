Stock Tracker

Open Source

A mobile stock and portfolio tracking application designed for monitoring asset prices, market trends, and trade positions on Android.

Android Java Finance Portfolio Open Source

About the App

Stock Tracker is an Android application built for investors and traders who want a lightweight, focused tool to track their portfolios. The app allows you to add assets, monitor live prices, and track your overall portfolio performance in real time.

Designed with a clean, data-dense interface, it prioritises the information you need without clutter. Ideal for tracking individual stocks, crypto, or any other ticker-based asset.

How to Use

Managing your investment holdings is straightforward:

1

Add Tickers

Search and add equities, ETFs, or market securities to your watchlist by ticker symbol.

2

Log Holdings

Input your acquired share quantities and average purchase prices to track investment cost basis.

3

Monitor Live Prices

Track real-time valuations, day-gain fluctuations, and percentage changes across positions.

4

Evaluate Net P&L

Review comprehensive unrealized profit/loss summaries and portfolio allocation charts.

Key Capabilities

Portfolio Overview

See your total portfolio value and performance at a glance with a clean dashboard.

Asset Tracking

Add stocks, ETFs, or crypto by ticker symbol and track prices in real time.

Market Trends

View price changes over time with simple trend indicators for each position.

Trade Positions

Log buy/sell positions and calculate your P&L for each held asset.
