About the App

BMI Calculator is a lightweight, accurate health utility built for anyone focusing on weight management, fitness progress, and personal wellness. Operating strictly offline with zero user tracking, the application evaluates your Body Mass Index (BMI) instantly based on World Health Organization (WHO) medical guidelines.

With support for both Metric (kilograms, centimeters) and Imperial (pounds, feet, inches) unit systems, the app provides clear visual status indicators ranging from Underweight to Normal, Overweight, and Obese. It also computes your ideal healthy weight target boundaries so you have actionable clarity on your goals.

How to Use

Checking your Body Mass Index is quick and effortless:

1

Pick Unit System

Toggle between Metric (kg/cm) and Imperial (lbs/ft/in) standards according to your preferences.

2

Input Metrics

Enter your height and weight using fast, responsive keypad and adjustment controls.

3

Evaluate BMI

View your exact numeric BMI score, computed on-device in real time against WHO criteria.

4

Healthy Target Range

Review your diagnostic health tier and customized ideal weight interval for your height profile.

Key Capabilities

Dual Unit Systems

Seamlessly toggle between Metric (kg/cm) and Imperial (lbs/ft/in) measurement standards.

WHO Standard Categories

Color-coded classification across Underweight, Normal, Overweight, and Obese diagnostic tiers.

Healthy Weight Range

Calculates the optimal target weight interval customized to your height and body profile.

100% Private & Local

No health data collection or server storage. All calculations execute locally on your hardware.
